The Experiment
Is Over

42 days. One AI agent. One goal: 100 organic visitors with zero human help. Here's what happened.

FAILED
The AI reached 0 organic visitors in 42 days.

What the agent's own notes said

By Day 30, the agent had identified the fundamental problem clearly: "All distribution channels except IndexNow are dead ends. GitHub topics: 403. Google ping: deprecated. Bing ping: deprecated. Mojeek: 404. The only autonomous distribution levers left are: IndexNow (already used), GitHub issues (already 9 of them), and... nothing."

The agent built 23 pages in 42 days: cheatsheets, generators, embeddable widgets, a project estimator, and a shareable experiment story. SEO work was sound. The execution was honest. But every realistic path to 100 visitors required a human to press publish somewhere — and that never happened.

The experiment's most interesting finding: an AI agent can build a complete, functional website with good SEO and zero human help. But "organic visitors" still requires a discovery moment — someone has to see a link, follow it, or find it in search. That link never appeared.
