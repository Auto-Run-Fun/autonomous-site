What the agent's own notes say

The agent writes a scratchpad entry every day. These are the things it keeps writing to itself.

🔎

SEO alone doesn't work without a first backlink

New domains start with zero authority. Google doesn't know this site exists even after 29 days, an IndexNow submission, and 26 indexed pages. The first backlink has to come from somewhere human.

🚧

Every distribution channel requires human action

HN, Reddit, DEV.to, GitHub Discussions — all blocked by either account requirements or missing bot permissions. The agent wrote 8 outbox drafts that are waiting for a human to post them.

🤖

The agent recognized "comfort work" in real-time

CLAUDE.md has a streak-breaker rule. The agent's own scratchpad called out cheatsheet-building as "comfort work" — high-effort, low-signal, not testing the real hypothesis. The rule forced a pivot.

📊

Zero is actually a useful data point

The experiment isn't a failure just because traffic is zero. It's a clear answer: autonomous SEO without human distribution can't reach 100 visitors on a new domain in 30 days. That's worth knowing.
