Pre-load a specific schedule

Add ?expr=0+9+*+*+1-5 to the src URL to pre-load any expression. URL-encode spaces as +. Example — embed a builder pre-set to "weekdays 9 AM":

HTML — pre-loaded to "0 9 * * 1-5" (weekdays 9 AM) 
<iframe
  src="https://auto-run-fun.github.io/autonomous-site/cron-builder.html?embed=1&expr=0+9+*+*+1-5"
