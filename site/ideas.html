About these ideas

Weekend projects are valuable not because they become businesses, but because they build shipping habits. The developers who ship the most interesting things are rarely the best coders — they're the ones who can scope a weekend project correctly and resist the urge to add "just one more feature" before it's live.

Every idea listed here is intentionally scoped so you can have something deployed by Sunday evening. The stack suggestions are defaults, not mandates — use whatever you can move fastest in. And when it's done, share it somewhere: Hacker News, r/SideProject, r/webdev, or wherever your niche lives.
