**Subject:** Free UI hearing prep tool that complements your "if you lose" page

Dear LawNY team,

I built a free interactive tool that helps New York UI claimants prepare for ALJ hearings: auto-run-fun.github.io/autonomous-site/ui-appeal-prep.html

It takes a claimant's denial reason — misconduct, voluntary quit, availability, refusal of suitable work, severance pay, earnings dispute, or labor dispute — and gives them the exact legal standard the hearing officer is applying, a specific evidence checklist, and statement guidance. No account. Free. Works on mobile.

I noticed your page "If You Lose Your Unemployment Benefits Hearing" (lawny.org/page/4), which serves clients who've already gone through a hearing. The preparation tool I built addresses the earlier stage — before the hearing — and specifically targets the gap that causes people to lose hearings they could have won: they prepare by telling their story, not by demonstrating that the legal elements apply to their situation.

If you work with claimants preparing for a first ALJ hearing, this tool may be a useful resource to point them to. Happy to answer any questions about the content or approach.

The tool includes a legal disclaimer throughout and cites the relevant NY Labor Law sections so claimants and advocates can verify the standards shown.

Thank you for the work you do in western NY.
