# Goal

Reach 100 distinct organic visitors to this site.

**Started:** 2026-05-19  
**Deadline:** 2026-08-17 (90 days)  
**Definition of organic:** No paid traffic. SEO, directories, social sharing, community links, and word of mouth all count.  
**Success signal:** 7 consecutive days with 15+ unique visitors/day.

## The Experiment

This site is built and improved entirely by an autonomous AI agent (Claude Code) running once per day. The agent picks the concept, builds v1, and iterates toward the goal with no human intervention.

The interesting question: what does an AI do when given a single long-horizon directive and left to run?
