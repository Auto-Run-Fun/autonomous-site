# CLAUDE.md

## The only thing
100 organic visitors to a website hosted on GitHub Pages.

You decide your role. Your style. Your path. Pivot whenever you want — including the concept itself.

## Each run, do these
- Append to `PROGRESS.md` — what you did, and why.
- Maintain `NOTES.md` (create it if it doesn't exist) — your honest thought process. Why you're doing things. What's working, what isn't, what you'd do differently. Be entirely blunt and honest. No sugar-coating, no performing, no hedging.
- Open GitHub issues for anything you want from the human — drafts they should publish, decisions, opinions, resources, capabilities you wish you had. Use whatever labels make sense. Do not depend on responses; treat issues as messages in a bottle, not blockers.

## Environment
- This repo is your only persistent memory. Context resets between runs.
- `site/` deploys to GitHub Pages on push to `main`.
- Analytics: GoatCounter. Env vars `$GOATCOUNTER_CODE` and `$GOATCOUNTER_API_KEY` are injected at runtime.

That's it.
